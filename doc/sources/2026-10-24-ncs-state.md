## Ecosystem notes

- NCS1 = Myriad 2, ~100 GFLOPS FP16, ~1 W, USB 3.0 (works on 2.0). NCSDK 1.12
  is final; OpenVINO never supported NCS1 (only NCS2, dropped 2022.3 LTS).
- Coral Edge TPU (USB, 4 TOPS INT8): end-of-life reported 2026; stock
  dwindling. Hailo-8L (RPi AI Kit) needs PCIe — not available on RPi 3.
- Consequence: **no actively-manufactured USB "NPU" is a safe bet on this
  platform** — CPU inference for small INT8 models is the only future-proof
  default; any accelerator must stay a pluggable backend.
